Headers

The wide artwork, at each platform's own stated pixel size so an upload is never resampled on the way in. The lockup can go left or right, in either ink, with or without the line underneath. Left is the safer default on all three platforms only if the artwork is not cropped; every one of them parks the avatar in the bottom-left corner, so on a short banner the right-hand setting is the one that stays clear of it.

Colour

The site is set in ink on paper and reverses wholesale for the dark theme. The magenta belongs to the artwork; it is not an interface colour and nothing is set in it.

Type

Two families, both self-hosted. Instrument Serif carries the name and the headings; everything else is Barlow Semi Condensed, with Barlow's regular width reserved for names that are not ours.

  • Instrument Serif Regular The wordmark and every heading. 400 only.
  • Barlow Semi Condensed Regular Interface, navigation and body copy. 400 and 500.
  • Barlow Semi Condensed ExtraLight Italic The line under the wordmark, and nothing else.
